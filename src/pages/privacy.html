Last updated: 22 October 2026. Outpost has no accounts and does not ask for personal information to read the site.

Advertising

Outpost may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. You can manage this at Google Ad Settings. Where the law requires consent, for example in the EEA and the UK, Google’s consent message is used before personalised ads are shown.

Hosting

Outpost is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives your IP address and request details such as the page and browser type when you load a page, and may keep them in standard server logs.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It works without cookies.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page loads.

Newsletter

If you subscribe by email, your address is handled by Buttondown and used to send the briefing. The sign-up form submits directly to Buttondown, so Buttondown’s own privacy policy applies to that step. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Browser storage

Outpost stores a few preferences in your browser only, and sends them nowhere: your colour theme, whether you prefer Read or Scan view, and which items you have ticked on the daily checklist.

Links to other sites

Stories link to outlets and tools run by others. They have their own privacy practices.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests about your data: open an issue on GitHub.
