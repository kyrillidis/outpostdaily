Outpost is a free daily briefing on cybersecurity, modern warfare technology and geopolitics and geodata, compiled automatically from open sources. Every story links to the outlet that reported it, so you can check the original in one click.

What you will find

  • Cyber: breaches, campaigns, advisories and research from CISA, vendors and the security press.
  • CVEs: the newest vulnerabilities CISA says are being exploited, with severity scores from NVD.
  • Warfare: drones, electronic warfare, missiles, air defence and the systems behind operations.
  • Geopolitics: strategy, the geo wars, GNSS jamming and spoofing, space and satellite security.
  • Today & Learn: the five stories that matter, a short checklist, and one concept per domain explained.
  • OSINT Toolbox and the glossary: tools for checking the news yourself, and plain-language definitions of the terms.

New here?

Use Headlines at the top of any page for a compact list, or Full for pictures and short descriptions. Each page has a collapsed “Key terms” panel, and the glossary explains every term. Press / to search and j or k to move between stories.

Questions

Independence and disclosure

Ranking is automatic and ignores who is mentioned: nobody can pay to be included or moved up. Outpost may show Google ads, and it promotes Cylist, a threat-intelligence triage tool made by Outpost’s publisher, in a labelled card. Outpost is not affiliated with any government, military or news organisation. Nothing here is legal, financial or operational advice.

Corrections and contact

Found an error or a broken link, or know a feed worth adding? Open an issue on GitHub. The code, ranking rules and source list are public at github.com/kyrillidis/outpostdaily under the MIT licence. You can also follow the RSS feed.
