Outpost is a free, daily briefing on cybersecurity, modern warfare and world news, assembled from open sources and published every morning. It is built for people who want to stay current and learn the field: every story links to the outlet that reported it, and each day includes one cyber concept and one warfare concept explained from that day’s reporting.

What you will find here

  • Cyber — breaches, campaigns, advisories and research from CISA, vendors and security press.
  • CVEs — the newest vulnerabilities CISA lists as exploited in the wild, with CVSS scores from NVD.
  • Warfare — operations, systems and analysis from defense press, think tanks and open-source investigators.
  • World — the general news that shapes the security picture.
  • Today & Learn — the five stories that matter, a defender checklist and two concepts to take away.
  • OSINT Toolbox — tools for checking the news yourself.

Independence

Outpost is not affiliated with any government, military, vendor or news organisation. Nothing here is investment, legal or operational advice.

Corrections and contact

Found an error, a broken source or a feed we should add? Open an issue on GitHub. The whole site, including the code that compiles each edition, is public. You can also follow along with the RSS feed.
